# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-21_

Vimse ("the app") is a life-organizer app for recipes and shopping lists, built to work offline-first with optional cloud sync.

## What we collect

- **Account info**: email address and password (via Supabase Auth) when you create an account, or OAuth identity info if you sign in with a third-party provider.
- **Profile info**: display name and avatar, if you choose to set them.
- **App content**: recipes, shopping lists, and related data you create in the app. This is stored locally on your device (SQLite) and synced to our backend (Supabase, hosted on AWS/EU infrastructure) when you're signed in and online, so your data is available across devices.
- **Recipe images**: stored locally on your device only.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or any usage tracking. The app has no ads or third-party trackers.

## How we use it

Solely to provide the app's core functionality: authenticating you, storing your data, and syncing it across your devices.

## Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, under row-level security so only you can access your own data.

## Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained until you delete it or delete your account. You can delete your account in the app.
